The sun shines bright on a field of green,
The finest stage that has ever been.
The crease is marked, the wickets stand,
A leather ball in the bowler's hand.
A rhythmic run, a sudden leap,
The secrets that the pitches keep.
The ball flies fast, a lethal spin,
The battle of wits is about to begin.
The willow swings, a perfect crack,
The fieldsmen race, there's no looking back.
It clears the rope, the crowd goes wild,
The timeless joy of man and child.
A diving catch, a sudden shout,
The umpire's finger—the batsman is out!
From tense last overs to victories sweet,
The game of cricket cannot be beat.
